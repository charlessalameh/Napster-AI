Al Noor International School - Admissions

Admissions for the 2027-2028 academic year open on 1 January 2027 and close on 31 March 2027.

Documents required: passport copy, Emirates ID, last two school reports, vaccination record, and 2 photos.

Students applying for Grade 1 and above sit an English and Maths assessment.

The registration fee is AED 500 and is non-refundable.

Contact: admissions@alnoor-demo.ae, phone 04 000 0000, Sunday to Thursday, 8am to 3pm.
