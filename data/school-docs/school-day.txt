Al Noor International School - The School Day

School hours: 7:30am to 2:30pm Monday to Thursday, and 7:30am to 11:30am on Friday.

The uniform is sold at the school shop Sunday to Thursday, 8am to 12pm.

Hot lunch costs AED 15 per day. Only healthy snacks are allowed and the school is nut-free.

A nurse is on campus during school hours.
