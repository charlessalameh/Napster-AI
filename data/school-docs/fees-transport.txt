Al Noor International School - Fees and Transport

Tuition per year:
- KG1 to KG2: AED 32,000
- Grades 1 to 5: AED 38,000
- Grades 6 to 12: AED 45,000

Fees are paid in three instalments: September, January and April.

Sibling discount: 10% for the second child and 15% for the third child.

School bus: covers Al Barsha, JVC, Dubai Marina and Arabian Ranches. The bus costs AED 7,000 per year.
